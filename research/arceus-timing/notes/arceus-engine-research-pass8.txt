2. OWNER TRACES
Day clock. 1D5D59C calls 24AC8C4(obj, 1/30) when obj+70 (a time controller) is absent.
24AC8C4 adds s0 to a double at +70 counting in-game minutes (hour index +78, 60 per hour,
wraps at 1440) while +68 is set and +7C/+7D are clear. Authored rate: 1 in-game minute per
real second at 30 FPS, 24 min per day. At 60 FPS the day passed in 12 min. HIGH-FPS BUG.

Event hold timers. 167F988 (x19 = x0) adds 1/30 to +190 while 168038C is true, caps it at a
configured threshold and resets it to 0 otherwise; the comparison feeds 168790C. Callees
168300C (+1A0 two-second check, +198 six-second threshold) and 1683528 (+19C) keep the same
kind of timer and are reached only through 167F988 -> 168790C. HIGH-FPS BUG.

Weather blend. 1B47D10 keeps three blend values at +24C/+250/+254 clamped 0..1. Fades add
-rate*1/30 (rate from 1B49780) at 1B47F5C/F94/FCC. Rises add config_rate*1/30 at 1B48A48,
1B48A94, 1B49004, 1B493F4, inside the direct-set branches through +220/+230. HIGH-FPS BUG.

Cave light. 1E1A25C (cave_light_change_easing) record x0+5E640: target +0, active +4,
progress +8, increment +C. A change stores increment 1/120 and adds it every call until
progress reaches 1: 4 s at 30 FPS, 2 s at 60. HIGH-FPS BUG.

Shared character movement 1982408 (1982930): displacement = velocity * rate * 1/30, then a
collision-aware sweep 19835E0, then * 29.99999 back to a velocity submitted through vtable
+100. The 1/30 is a probe horizon and cancels out. NOT A SPEED BUG. This is why player
movement looks right at high FPS.

Shared acceleration 198D7E4: returns a large structure through x8 (Havok character input
style: defaults 1.0/100.0, up and forward vectors, result vector at +40). In the airborne
branch it builds -g(413A0F8) * factor(+BE8) * rate(198E188) / 30, adds an accel term *
1/30 * rate and clamps against +698. Whether +40 is a new velocity or a change in velocity
was not settled. UNRESOLVED. Next: a read-only probe logging out+40 and obj+6B0 per frame
while falling, at two frame rates.

Player controller 1A08560 and method 1A0F278: acceleration * 1/30 added to velocity with a
max-speed clamp, and 1/30 passed to helper 1713904. Effect likely limited to reaching top
speed or turning sooner. UNRESOLVED, not reported by the user.
